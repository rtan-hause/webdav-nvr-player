CSplit · chosen

The day's clips in a scrolling column on the left, video and timeline on the right. On a phone the clip list moves under the timeline.

Most picture per pixel and closest to the Reolink phone app. The controls cover the bottom of the picture while they're showing.

Try it

  • Click or drag on a lane to move the playhead across the day. Tapping a lane also switches to that camera.
  • Zoom with − and +, the scroll wheel (zooms where the pointer is), or a pinch. The day starts fully zoomed out.
  • When zoomed in, drag the time axis or the thin overview strip at the top to move along the day. The amber box in the strip shows the part on screen.
  • While playing, the view pages forward so the playhead stays visible. The hatched area is time that hasn't happened yet.
  • Theater mode: press F, double-click the video, or use the expand button next to the zoom control. The video takes over the screen, controls fade while playing, and the clip list slides up from Clips. Esc or the same button brings you back to Split, at the same moment.
  • Keys: Space play/pause, ← → 10 s, [ ] previous/next clip, 1–4 camera.
  • Press play just before a gap. Playback skips to the next clip and says how much it skipped.
  • The codec badge really checks your browser for HEVC support.

Decided

  • Layout C, with the clip list on the left on desktop.
  • Every clip is listed, newest first, with a toggle for oldest first.
  • The playhead moves across the day, and the day is zoomable.
  • Colours stay for now and may change later.
